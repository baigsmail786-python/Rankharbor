How Beginners Use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO

Learn how to use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO beginners. Verify your site, submit your sitemap, read search data, fix crawl errors, and find keywords that bring readers.

how to use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO beginners starts with one free account and one website that you verify. You add your site and Bing checks that you own it. Bing shows you the searches that bring visitors.

Most beginners verify the site and never open the tool again. They miss the crawl errors and the search data that Bing gives free. They lose traffic from Bing readers that their rivals win.

You will finish this guide with a simple Bing routine. You will submit your sitemap and check your index each week. You will fix crawl errors early and find the searches that bring readers.

Create Your Account and Verify Your Site

You open Bing Webmaster Tools and you sign in with a Microsoft account. You add your site address and Bing asks you to prove that you own the site. You pick the XML file method or the meta tag method and you follow the steps that Bing shows.

how to use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO beginners means you verify with the method that fits your site. A site on WordPress takes the meta tag in the SEO settings and a site on GitHub Pages takes the HTML file in the root folder. You save the change and you click verify and Bing confirms the site in seconds.

Ayesha runs a small bakery blog in Lahore and she creates a Bing Webmaster Tools account on a Monday. She adds her blog address and she picks the meta tag method in her site settings. She clicks verify and Bing confirms her blog and she opens the dashboard for the first time.

Submit Your Sitemap and Check the Index

You submit your sitemap so Bing can find every page on your site. You open the sitemaps page and you type the address of your sitemap file. Bing reads the file and lists the pages that it found.

You check the index page next and you read how many pages Bing holds in its index. You compare that number with the pages that your site holds. You note the pages that Bing missed and you check each one for a crawl block.

Bilal manages a clothing store site in Karachi and he submits his sitemap on a Tuesday. Bing reads the file and lists the pages that it found. He checks the index page and he sees that two product pages sit outside the index. He fixes the missing links on those pages and he asks Bing to crawl them again.

Read the Search Performance Report

You open the search performance report and you read the queries that bring clicks to your site. The report shows you the words that readers type and the pages that win the clicks. You read the trend across the last weeks so you see which pages grow and which pages fade.

how to use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO beginners means you sort the report by clicks and you read the top rows first. You find the queries that sit near the top of the results and you improve those pages first. You write new pages for the queries that bring impressions and earn no clicks yet.

Sana writes travel guides in Faisalabad and she opens the search performance report on a Wednesday. She reads the queries that bring clicks to her hill station guides. She finds one query that brings many impressions and earns no clicks. She rewrites the title of that guide and the clicks start to grow.

Use URL Inspection and IndexNow for Fast Indexing

You open URL inspection when a new page stays outside the index for days. You type the page address into the box and Bing shows you the crawl status and the index status. You read the errors that block the page and you fix them before you ask for a new crawl.

You use IndexNow when you publish a new page or change an old one. You send one ping to Bing and Bing crawls the page much sooner than a normal crawl. You save time on every new page because you never wait weeks for the next crawl.

Usman runs a tuition centre site in Rawalpindi and he publishes a new maths fee guide on a Thursday. He opens URL inspection and he types the guide address into the box. Bing shows that the page sits outside the index and he sends an IndexNow ping. Bing crawls the guide the same day and the guide starts to win clicks.

Find Keyword Ideas With the Keyword Research Tool

You open the keyword research tool and you type one word that names your topic. The tool shows you the phrases that Bing readers type around that word. You read the list and you pick the phrases that match pages you can write with real skill.

You check the volume beside each phrase before you pick it. A phrase with high volume needs a strong page and a phrase with low volume fits a new page. You pick the phrases that your site can win and you save them in a sheet.

Hina manages a home decor blog in Multan and she opens the keyword research tool on a Friday. She types velvet sofa into the box and she reads the phrases that the tool shows. She picks the phrases that match her care guides and she reads How Beginners Use Moz for SEO and What Each Tool Does and she writes one new guide for each phrase that her site can win.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bing Webmaster Tools?

Bing Webmaster Tools is a free tool from Microsoft that shows how Bing sees your website. You add your site and you verify that you own it. Bing shows you the searches that bring clicks and the pages that sit in its index. You use the tool to find crawl errors and submit sitemaps and check your rankings on Bing.

Is Bing Webmaster Tools free?

Bing Webmaster Tools is free for every site owner with a Microsoft account. You create the account and you verify your site and you use every report at no cost. You submit sitemaps and you inspect pages and you research keywords without a paid plan. You pay nothing and you keep the tool for the life of your site.

How do I verify my site in Bing Webmaster Tools?

You verify your site by adding the site address and picking the XML file method or the meta tag method. You place the file in the root folder of your site or you add the meta tag to the home page. You click verify in the dashboard and Bing confirms the site in seconds. You keep the file or the tag in place so Bing never loses the check.

How do I submit a sitemap to Bing?

You submit a sitemap by opening the sitemaps page in the dashboard and typing the address of your sitemap file. Bing reads the file and lists the pages that it found. You check the list and you fix the pages that Bing missed. You submit the sitemap again after you add new pages to your site.

How do beginners use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO?

how to use Bing Webmaster Tools for SEO beginners starts with one free account and one verified site. You submit your sitemap and you check the index each week. You read the search performance report and you fix the crawl errors first. You use the keyword research tool to find the phrases that your site can win.

What is IndexNow and how does it work?

IndexNow is a protocol that tells Bing about a new page or a changed page the moment you publish it. You send one ping to Bing and Bing crawls the page much sooner than a normal crawl. You set it up once on your site and every new page pings Bing on its own. You save days of waiting on every page that you publish.

How is Bing Webmaster Tools different from Google Search Console?

Bing Webmaster Tools shows your data on Bing and Google Search Console shows your data on Google. Bing adds the keyword research tool and IndexNow and a free site scan that Search Console lacks. You use both tools because each search engine reads your site in its own way. You fix the errors that each tool finds and your site wins traffic from both engines.

How often should I check Bing Webmaster Tools?

You should check Bing Webmaster Tools once each week and keep the session short. You should read the search performance report and fix the crawl errors first. You should submit the sitemap again after you add new pages. A weekly check gives you enough data to plan ahead without wasting time on endless checks.

You read the search data and you fix the errors first and you build pages around the words that real readers type.
