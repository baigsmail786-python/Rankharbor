What Are Dofollow and Nofollow Links in SEO and When Do You Use Each?

Learn what dofollow and nofollow links mean, how each one passes value, and when to use each so your link profile stays natural and earns trust.

A dofollow link passes ranking value from one site to another, while a nofollow link tells Google to pass no ranking value through it. This guide answers what are dofollow and nofollow links in SEO and shows when you use each one.

Most site owners chase dofollow links only and ignore nofollow links that still bring visitors. They buy dofollow links from weak sites and harm their own rankings. This habit wastes cash on links that add risk instead of trust.

You will learn how each link type works, how Google reads the tags, and how to mix both so your site earns trust and traffic.

How Dofollow Links Pass Ranking Value Between Sites

A dofollow link acts as a vote that tells Google the linked page deserves trust. Google follows the link and it passes ranking value from the source page to the target page. A page with strong dofollow links from trusted sites climbs above rivals with weak links.

Dofollow links suit earned mentions because a writer links a guide that helped their readers. You earn these links by writing guides that other writers cite in their own work. This natural flow explains why strong pages attract dofollow links without begging for them.

Ayesha runs a small salon blog in Gulberg and she wrote a clear guide about hair care at home. A beauty magazine linked her guide as a dofollow link because it helped their readers. Her guide now ranks higher because Google counts that trusted vote in her favour.

How Nofollow Links Work And Why They Still Bring Value

A nofollow link carries a tag that tells Google to pass no ranking value through it. Google still follows the link for discovery and readers still click it to reach your site. This guide explains what are dofollow and nofollow links in SEO so you can judge each link by its real worth.

Nofollow links suit comments, ads, and pages you cannot vouch for because they stop value from flowing to weak targets. You mark paid links nofollow so Google sees no bought vote behind them. This honest tag keeps your site safe from penalties that bought links invite.

Bilal owns a tyre shop in Saddar and he left helpful replies on car forums with a link to his tyre guide. His forum links carried the nofollow tag yet drivers clicked them and called his shop. His calls grew because readers trusted his advice even though Google passed no ranking value.

How Rel Sponsored And Rel UGC Tags Fit Beside Nofollow

Google added rel sponsored and rel ugc beside nofollow so each link carries a clear hint about its purpose. Paid links wear the sponsored tag and reader links wear the ugc tag. A clear hint helps Google judge the link fairly instead of guessing its purpose.

You use rel sponsored when cash or a free product bought the link on a page. You use rel ugc when a reader placed the link in a comment or a forum thread. You use plain nofollow when the link fits neither case yet still needs no ranking value.

Sara runs a home bakery in DHA and she paid a food blogger to review her cakes with a link to her order page. She asked the blogger to mark the link rel sponsored so Google reads it as a paid mention. Her site stayed safe because the honest tag showed Google that no bought vote hid behind the link.

When To Use Dofollow Links And When To Use Nofollow Links

You use dofollow links for earned mentions that point to pages you trust and value. You use nofollow links for paid mentions, comments, and pages you cannot vouch for. This split shows the dofollow vs nofollow difference in action because real sites mix both kinds of links.

A site that earns links from guides, news, and partners gains dofollow votes that lift its rank. A site that marks ads and comments nofollow shows Google that it plays fair with paid links. This balance answers what are dofollow and nofollow links in SEO in practice because each tag fits a distinct kind of link.

Ahmed owns a small gym in Faisalabad and he earned a dofollow link when a health site cited his training guide. He marked the links in his own blog comments nofollow because readers placed them there. His gym site grew because Google saw earned votes beside honest tags.

How To Check Links And Build A Healthy Mix

You check a link by opening the page source and looking for rel nofollow beside the link address. Browser tools also highlight dofollow and nofollow links in colour so you can scan a page fast. This quick check shows whether a link passes value before you chase it.

You build a healthy mix by earning dofollow links from guides and letting nofollow links flow from comments and forums. You never buy dofollow links because Google punishes bought votes when it finds them. This patient path grows trust that lasts instead of rank that fades fast.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do nofollow links help SEO at all?

Nofollow links help SEO in a roundabout way because they bring visitors who read, share, and link your pages with dofollow links. They also show Google that real people talk about your site across the web. You welcome them beside dofollow links because traffic and mentions both build your name.

What happens if I mark every link nofollow?

A page that marks every link nofollow looks guarded because real sites pass value to pages they trust. Google reads the pattern and it may treat your outbound links with less trust. You keep dofollow for earned mentions and nofollow for paid links and comments.

Should I ask a site to change my nofollow link to dofollow?

You can ask politely when your guide truly helps their readers and the link fits their page. Many owners agree when they see the value your page adds for their visitors. You accept a nofollow link with grace when they refuse because the visit and the mention still help.

What is the difference between rel sponsored and rel nofollow?

Rel sponsored marks a link that cash or a free product bought, while rel nofollow marks a link that passes no value for any other reason. Google treats both as hints that pass no ranking value. You pick sponsored for paid mentions and nofollow for links you cannot vouch for.

Do blog comments with nofollow links still bring traffic?

Blog comments bring traffic when your reply helps the readers and your link leads to a guide they need. Readers click links from writers who add real value to the talk. You write comments that teach something and you link only when the guide fits the thread.

Can too many dofollow links harm my site?

Too many dofollow links harm your site when they come from bought placements or weak sites that Google distrusts. A natural profile mixes dofollow votes from trusted sites with nofollow links from comments and forums. You earn links slowly from real sites and you skip any seller who offers fast bulk links.

How do I find which of my backlinks are dofollow?

You open a backlink tool and you filter your links by type to see which ones pass value. Free checkers show the tag beside each link, and paid tools list the mix across your whole profile. You review the list each month and you note which pages earn the most dofollow votes.

Should new websites worry about nofollow links?

A new website should welcome nofollow links because early mentions bring the first visitors and the first signs of trust. Google sees a mix of link types as a natural sign that people talk about a real site. You focus on strong guides and honest outreach, and the dofollow votes follow as your name grows.
