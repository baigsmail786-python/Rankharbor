What Is Keyword Difficulty in SEO and How Do You Check It?

Learn what keyword difficulty means, how Ahrefs and Semrush score it, and how new sites pick low difficulty phrases they can win.

Keyword difficulty scores how hard it feels to rank for one phrase. A high score means strong pages already hold the top spots. When you ask what is keyword difficulty in SEO you ask how that score guides your choice of phrase.

Many beginners chase high volume phrases and they ignore the difficulty score. Strong rivals hold those spots and a new site cannot pass them.

After this guide you will read a difficulty score and pick phrases you can win. You will balance volume against difficulty and build pages that rank. Your new pages will earn visits from phrases that fit your site strength.

A Difficulty Score Measures the Strength of Rivals

A difficulty score reads the pages that already rank for a phrase. The score rises when those pages hold strong links and deep content. When you ask what is keyword difficulty in SEO you ask how strong those rivals stand. A low score means weak rivals and a clear opening for a new page.

Tools build the score from links, content depth and site strength of the top pages. Each tool weighs these signs in its own way and scores differ across tools. Owners compare scores from one tool at a time and they avoid mixing tools in one choice. This steady method keeps each choice fair across phrases.

Sara runs a recipe site in Lahore and she checked scores before she wrote each guide. Her first guide chased a high score phrase and it gained no visits. Sara picked a low score phrase next and her guide reached the first page. Her visits grew because she matched each phrase to her site strength.

How Tools Check Keyword Difficulty

Ahrefs shows a difficulty score beside each phrase in its keyword tool. The score reads the links of the top pages and it ranks the phrase from easy to hard. Owners type a phrase and they read the score before they plan a page. This quick read stops wasted work on phrases a new site cannot win.

Semrush shows its own score and owners compare it with the Ahrefs score. Owners who ask what is keyword difficulty in SEO learn that the two scores differ by design. Owners pick one tool and they judge every phrase with that same tool. This single tool habit keeps scores fair across a full keyword list.

Omar runs a travel site in Karachi and he checked each phrase in one tool only. His early lists mixed scores from two tools and his choices felt random. Omar settled on one tool and his phrase choices turned steady. His guides now target phrases that fit his site and his visits grow each month.

New Sites Win With Low Difficulty Phrases

A new site holds few links and it cannot pass strong rivals on hard phrases. Low difficulty phrases offer a fair fight and a real chance to rank. Owners start with these phrases and they build links and trust with each win. This patient start explains why small sites grow through easy wins first.

Owners who skip easy phrases and chase hard ones wait months for a first visit. Each easy win adds a page that ranks and it earns links from readers. Those wins lift the site strength and they open the door to harder phrases later. This ladder keeps growth steady instead of stalled.

Ayesha manages a job board in Islamabad and her new site held no links at all. Her first guides chased hard phrases and they gained no visits. Ayesha switched to low difficulty phrases for local jobs and her guides reached the first page. Her job board now earns visits daily and she targets harder phrases with care.

Difficulty and Volume Must Balance Together

A high volume phrase with high difficulty tempts owners with big traffic. That traffic stays out of reach when strong rivals hold the spots. A phrase with low volume and low difficulty brings few visits but it ranks fast. Owners weigh both signs together and they pick phrases that balance reach with rank.

Volume alone misleads owners because it hides the strength of rivals. Difficulty alone misleads too because it hides the size of the prize. A balanced phrase holds enough volume to count and a score low enough to win. Writers sort phrases by both signs and they plan pages for the balanced ones first.

Bilal owns a furniture store in Faisalabad and he chased a high volume phrase for months. Strong rivals held the top spots and his page gained no visits. Bilal picked a balanced phrase with fair volume and a low score. His page reached the first page and his store gained steady visits from buyers.

Pick Target Phrases With a Simple Score Check

Owners pick targets by listing phrases with volume and difficulty side by side. The list shows which phrases offer visits that a site can win. Owners drop phrases with scores above their site strength. This short list turns a long keyword dump into a clear plan.

A fresh site sets a low score limit and it raises that limit as links grow. Each win adds strength and it opens the next band of phrases. Owners revisit the list each month and they move new phrases into the plan. This monthly review keeps targets matched to real site strength.

Hira runs a coaching site in Multan and she set a low score limit for her first guides. Her early guides won easy phrases and her site gained links from readers. Hira raised her limit each month as her site grew stronger. Her guides now target harder phrases and enrolments rise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is keyword difficulty in simple terms?

Keyword difficulty scores how hard it feels to rank for one phrase. Owners who ask what is keyword difficulty in SEO learn that the score reads the strength of pages that already rank. A low score means weak rivals and a clear opening for a new page.

Which tool shows keyword difficulty for free?

Several tools show a free score for a few phrases each day. Owners type a phrase and they read the score beside it before they plan a page. Free limits suit small lists and paid plans suit large keyword research.

What is a good keyword difficulty score for a new site?

A new site wins most with low scores that match its weak link profile. Owners set a low limit at first and they raise it as links grow. Each win adds strength and it opens the next band of phrases.

Does search volume change keyword difficulty?

Volume never changes the score because the score reads rival strength, not search counts. A phrase can hold high volume and a low score when rivals stay weak. Owners weigh both signs together before they pick a target.

Why do difficulty scores differ across tools?

Scores differ because each tool weighs links, content and site strength in its own way. One tool may rank a phrase as easy while another calls it hard. Owners pick one tool and they judge every phrase with that same tool.

Can a new site rank for a hard phrase?

A new site rarely ranks for a hard phrase because strong rivals hold the top spots. The page needs deep content and strong links to pass them. Owners build strength through easy wins first and they return to hard phrases later.

How often should owners check keyword difficulty?

Owners check difficulty each time they plan a new page or a new cluster. Rival strength shifts as rivals publish and earn links. A monthly review keeps each target matched to real site strength.

Does low difficulty mean low traffic?

Low difficulty never means low traffic because many easy phrases still draw steady visits. A cluster of easy phrases can bring more visits than one hard phrase. Owners add up the visits across a cluster before they judge its worth.
