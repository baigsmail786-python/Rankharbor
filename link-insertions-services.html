Link Insertions

Short answer: A link insertion (or niche edit) adds a contextual link to your site inside an article that already exists on another website. Because the host page is already published and often already indexed, insertions complement guest posting in a balanced link-building campaign.

Link insertions (also called niche edits) add a contextual link to your site inside an existing article on another website. Because the host page already exists — and is often already indexed and receiving traffic — insertions can complement guest posting in a balanced campaign.

How insertions differ from guest posts

A guest post creates a new article; an insertion adds your link to an article that already exists. Insertions suit supporting established content, while guest posts suit new topics and thought leadership. Many campaigns use both.

Existing-content & editorial review

We review the host article itself: does it genuinely relate to your target page, is it indexed, does it read naturally, and would the link help its readers? The publisher or site owner must agree editorially — we do not use hacked or unauthorized placements.

Destination page relevance

Your destination page must earn the link: the anchor text and surrounding sentence have to make sense to a reader arriving from the host article.

Verification & limitations

Every insertion is verified live and logged. Honest limitations: host sites control their own content, so edits can later change; terms are agreed per placement and confirmed in your report.

Link Insertions — quick answers

What is the difference between a link insertion and a guest post?

A guest post creates a brand-new article containing your link. A link insertion adds your link into an article that already exists. Guest posts suit new topics; insertions suit strengthening pages that already exist.

How much does a link insertion cost?

At RankHarbor, a single guest post or link placement is $100. Link insertions can also be included in monthly packages — PKR 25,000 to PKR 100,000 per month in Pakistan, or $150 to $600 per month internationally — with scope confirmed in your proposal.

Are link insertions safe?

Insertions are a normal part of link building when they are placed editorially, on relevant pages, with the site owner's agreement. RankHarbor never uses hacked or unauthorized placements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are link insertions safe?
Done editorially, on relevant pages, with the site owner's agreement, insertions are a normal part of link building. Unauthorized or hacked placements are never used.
Guest post or insertion — which is better?
Neither is universally better. Guest posts build new content and topics; insertions leverage existing authority. The right mix depends on your pages and goals.
Can I choose the host pages?
Candidate host pages are shared for approval before placement on standard campaigns.
