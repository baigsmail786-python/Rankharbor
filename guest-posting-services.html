Guest Posting

Short answer: Guest posting is publishing an original article on another relevant website, with a natural contextual link back to your site. RankHarbor researches publishers in your niche, writes the article to the publication's guidelines, and reports the live URL of every post.

Our guest posting service places original, genuinely useful articles on relevant third-party websites, with a natural contextual link back to your site. Every placement starts with publisher research — not a pre-bought list applied to every client. International guest posts are priced at $100 per link — see the pricing section on our homepage.

Content and editorial workflow

Topics are pitched to fit each publication's audience first. Articles are written to the site's guidelines, reviewed editorially by the publisher, and revised if needed before going live.

  • Topic ideation matched to the host audience
  • Original draft — no spun or recycled content
  • Editorial review and revision rounds
  • Natural, in-content link placement

Publisher research & topic relevance

We research publications in your niche, verify their editorial standards and audience, and only pitch topics that make sense for their readers. Relevance is checked at both site level and article level.

Content quality standards

Articles must stand on their own as useful content: accurate, well-structured, written for humans, and free of promotional stuffing. The link supports the article — the article is not built around the link.

Placement process

Research → pitch → draft → editorial review → publication → live-URL verification. You receive the live URL and placement details for every post.

Guest Posting — quick answers

How much does a guest post cost?

At RankHarbor, an international guest post or link is $100 per placement. Guest posting can also be included inside monthly SEO packages — PKR 25,000 to PKR 100,000 per month in Pakistan, or $150 to $600 per month internationally.

How long does it take for a guest post to go live?

Editorial timelines vary by publication, and a post typically goes live a few weeks after approval. The exact expectation is set per campaign — publication dates are never guaranteed to the day, because the publisher controls its own schedule.

Can I approve the websites before you publish?

Yes. On standard campaigns, publisher candidates are shared for your approval before outreach, and you receive the live URL, anchor text and target page for every published post.
