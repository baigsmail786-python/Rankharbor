On-Page SEO — quick answers

What is the difference between on-page SEO and technical SEO?

Technical SEO is about whether search engines can crawl and index your site correctly. On-page SEO is about what each page says — its title, headings, content quality and internal links — and whether it satisfies the searcher's intent.
