How Beginners Use Ahrefs Free Tools for Their First SEO Check

Learn the free Ahrefs tools step by step. Check backlinks, find keywords, audit your site free, and build a weekly SEO routine.

how to use Ahrefs free tools for beginners means you open the free tools on the Ahrefs site and you check your site without a paid plan. You start with the free backlink checker and you read the links that point to your site. You then open the free keyword generator and you collect ideas for posts.

Most beginners jump to a paid trial and they skip the free checks that show useful data. They collect numbers and they act on none of them.

You will finish your first check with a short list of backlinks to study and keywords to target. That list gives your next posts clear direction.

Start With the Free Backlink Checker

You open the free backlink checker on the Ahrefs site and you type your own domain into the box. The tool shows the top one hundred backlinks that point to your site and the domain rating for each linking site. You read the list and you note the sites that link to your best pages because those links show what Google trusts about your site.

You then check the anchor text for each backlink and you look for the words that other sites use when they link to you. You compare the anchor text with the topic of the linked page and you spot the pages that earn links with wrong words. You plan a small edit to the title so the page matches the anchor text that already works.

Ayesha runs a small bakery blog in Lahore and she opens the free backlink checker for her own site. She finds that a food forum links to her naan recipe with the words soft naan recipe. She adds that phrase to the title and her recipe post climbs in search within weeks.

Find Keyword Ideas With the Free Keyword Generator

You open the free keyword generator and you type a seed topic that matches your niche. The tool returns related phrases and it shows a difficulty score for each phrase. You pick the phrases that match posts you can write with real knowledge.

how to use Ahrefs free tools for beginners also means you read the difficulty score beside each phrase before you commit to a topic. You choose phrases with lower difficulty when your site is new because a new site wins easier battles first. You save the promising phrases in a simple sheet and you return to it when you plan the next month.

Bilal manages a clothing store site in Karachi and he types winter shawls into the free keyword generator. He finds a phrase about soft winter shawls with low difficulty. He writes a guide around that phrase and his product page gains visitors from search.

Connect Ahrefs Webmaster Tools for Your Own Site

You sign up for Ahrefs Webmaster Tools free and you verify your own website with a simple code. The tool audits your site and it lists the health score and the errors that hurt crawling. You open the site audit first because it shows the broken links and missing tags that you can fix at once.

You then open the Site Explorer view for your verified site and you read the backlinks and the top pages in one place. You compare the pages that earn links with the pages that earn no links and you spot the content that deserves more links. You add links from strong pages to weak pages because those links pass value to the pages that need it.

Sana writes travel guides in Faisalabad and she verifies her site in Ahrefs Webmaster Tools. She finds that several guide pages hold broken links after a theme change. She fixes the links and she adds internal links from her popular posts and her guides rank better within weeks.

Compare Free Tools With the Paid Plans

how to use Ahrefs free tools for beginners includes a clear look at the limits of the free tools before you consider a paid plan. The free backlink checker shows only the top one hundred backlinks and it hides the full history of each link. The free keyword generator shows a limited list of ideas and it hides the full volume data.

You weigh those limits against the needs of your site and you decide if the paid plan earns its cost. You list the checks that you run each week and you mark the checks that the free tools cover. You upgrade only when a paid feature fills a gap that blocks your work.

Usman runs a tuition centre site in Rawalpindi and he compares the free tools with the paid plan for one month. He finds that the free tools cover his weekly checks. He keeps the free tools and he spends his budget on new content.

Turn the First Check Into a Weekly Routine

You turn the first check into a weekly routine because a single check shows a snapshot and a routine shows a trend. You pick one fixed day and you run the backlink checker and the keyword generator in one session. You write down three actions and you finish them before the next session starts.

You study the new links that appear each week and you note the topics that earn them. You write more posts on the topics that earn links because those topics prove their appeal. You repeat the pattern that works because the data shows what readers and other sites already value.

Hina manages a home decor blog in Multan and she follows a Friday routine with the free Ahrefs tools every week. She checks new backlinks and she picks one keyword idea for the next post. She records each change in a simple notebook and her traffic grows each month.
