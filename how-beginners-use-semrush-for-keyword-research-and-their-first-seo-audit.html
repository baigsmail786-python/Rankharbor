How Beginners Use Semrush for Keyword Research and Their First SEO Audit

Learn how beginners use Semrush for keyword research and their first SEO audit. Find low difficulty phrases, read the difficulty score, and fix the errors that block your pages.

how to use Semrush for SEO beginners starts with a free account and one clear goal. You open the Keyword Magic Tool and you type a seed topic. The tool shows related phrases with difficulty scores.

Most beginners chase the phrase with the highest volume. They skip the difficulty score and they pick phrases that big sites own. They run a site audit and they ignore the errors that block their pages.

You will finish this guide with a simple routine for keyword research and your first audit. You will pick phrases that a new site can win. You will fix the errors that matter most.

Create a Free Account and Set One Clear Goal

You create a free Semrush account and you set one clear goal for the week. A free account gives you a small number of searches each day and that limit keeps a beginner focused. You pick one page on your site and you name the phrase that page must win.

You open the Semrush dashboard and you learn the names of the main tools before you run a search. The Keyword Magic Tool finds phrases and the Site Audit tool checks your pages for errors. You learn the layout now so your first real search feels calm and quick.

Ayesha runs a small bakery blog in Lahore and she creates a free Semrush account on a Monday. She picks her naan recipe page and she names the phrase that the page must win. She writes that goal in her notebook and she runs her first search with a clear aim.

Find Phrases in the Keyword Magic Tool

You type a seed topic into the Keyword Magic Tool and you read the list of related phrases. The tool groups the phrases by topic and it shows the volume beside each phrase. You save the phrases that fit posts you can write with real skill.

how to use Semrush for SEO beginners also means you filter the list for longer phrases that name a clear need. A long phrase like best soft naan recipe at home tells you what the reader wants. You write a post that answers that phrase in full because a full answer earns the click.

Bilal manages a clothing store site in Karachi and he types winter shawls into the Keyword Magic Tool. He filters the list for longer phrases and he finds one about soft winter shawls for gifts. He saves that phrase and he writes a gift guide around it.

Read the Difficulty Score Before You Pick a Phrase

You read the difficulty score beside each phrase before you commit to a topic. The score runs from zero to one hundred and a low score means a new site can compete. You treat a score under thirty as a green light for a small site.

You compare the difficulty score with the volume before you choose a phrase. A phrase with high volume and high difficulty wastes your effort on a new site. You pick phrases with modest volume and low difficulty because those phrases bring visitors that you can win.

Sana writes travel guides in Faisalabad and she checks the difficulty score for hill stations. She finds a long phrase about the best time to visit a hill station in winter with a low score. She writes a guide around that phrase and her page starts to receive visitors from search.

Run Your First Site Audit and Fix the Errors

how to use Semrush for SEO beginners includes a first site audit that shows the errors on your pages. You open the Site Audit tool and you type your site address into the setup box. The tool crawls your pages and it lists errors and warnings in one clear report.

You fix the errors in order of harm and you start with broken links and slow pages. A broken link sends visitors to a dead page and a slow page makes visitors leave fast. You fix the broken links first and you compress the large images that slow your pages.

Usman runs a tuition centre site in Rawalpindi and he runs his first site audit on a Sunday. The audit shows broken links on his course pages and large images that slow the site. He fixes the links and the images and he reads technical SEO fixes for beginners before he reruns the audit.

Compare Semrush With Ahrefs and Build a Weekly Routine

You compare Semrush with Ahrefs on the same phrase and you keep the tool that fits your budget. Semrush gives beginners quick ideas and clear difficulty scores with a simple layout. Ahrefs gives deeper data and stronger backlink tools but its free tools show less detail.

You build a weekly routine that keeps your keyword work short and steady. You run keyword research once each week and you pick one seed topic per session. You save the best phrases in a sheet and you write one post from that sheet each week.

Hina manages a home decor blog in Multan and she compares Semrush with Ahrefs each month. She keeps Semrush for her weekly keyword work because its layout feels simple. She writes one post per week from her keyword sheet and she runs a site audit each month.
