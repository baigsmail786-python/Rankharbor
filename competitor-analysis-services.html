Competitor Backlink Analysis

Short answer: Competitor backlink analysis compares your backlink profile with the sites that outrank you, showing which websites link to them but not to you. RankHarbor turns that gap into a prioritized outreach target list you can act on.

Competitor backlink analysis reverse-engineers what is working in your market: which sites link to competitors, which content earns those links, and where your profile has gaps worth closing.

What the analysis covers

We profile your main organic competitors and compare their link profiles with yours:

  • Referring domains competitors have that you do not
  • The pages and assets earning their links
  • Anchor-text and link-type patterns in your niche
  • Realistic targets ranked by relevance and attainability

From analysis to action

The output is not just charts — it is a prioritized outreach and content target list that can feed directly into a link-building campaign.

Competitor Backlink Analysis — quick answers

What does competitor backlink analysis include?

It includes the referring domains competitors have that you do not, the pages and assets earning their links, anchor-text and link-type patterns in your niche, and realistic targets ranked by relevance and attainability.

How many competitors are analyzed?

Typically three to five direct organic competitors — the sites ranking for your target keywords — agreed with you at kickoff.

How much does competitor backlink analysis cost?

Competitor analysis is included in the Growth and Premium monthly packages (PKR 50,000 and PKR 100,000 per month in Pakistan; $300 and $600 per month internationally), and a Full SEO Audit (PKR 15,000 one-time) includes a competitor overview.
