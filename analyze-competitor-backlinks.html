How to Analyze Competitor Backlinks (And Turn Gaps Into Targets)

Competitor backlink analysis shows which sites link to rivals but not you. Here is the workflow: pick true competitors, extract gaps, qualify targets and build an outreach list.

Pick the right competitors

Your SEO competitors are the sites ranking for your target keywords — not always your business rivals. Pick three to five that consistently outrank you for your priority clusters; their link profiles show what your market rewards.

Extract the link gap

Using a backlink tool, list referring domains that link to two or more competitors but not to you. These sites have already demonstrated they link to companies like yours — the highest-probability outreach pool that exists.

Qualify before you outreach

A gap list is raw material, not a target list. Qualify each domain:

  • Relevance — does it cover your niche or your buyers' problems?
  • Quality — editorial standards, real audience, sane outbound patterns
  • Link type — editorial article, resource page, directory, partner page?
  • Replicability — what earned the link: content, data, a tool, a relationship?

Study their linkable assets

Look at which competitor pages attract the most links. Original research, free tools, templates and definitive guides dominate. If competitors earn links with an asset type you lack, that is a content investment signal, not just an outreach task.

Turn it into a campaign

Rank qualified targets by relevance and attainability, and work them in order — pitching guest posts, requesting insertions where appropriate and building the assets gaps reveal.
