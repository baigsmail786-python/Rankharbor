Why Islamabad businesses need to be found on Google

Islamabad is a city of offices, markets and new startups, and its customers search on Google before they choose a service. A tech startup in Blue Area, a consultancy in F-7 Markaz, a cafe in F-10, a clinic in G-9, a real estate office in Bahria Town and a training institute in I-8 Markaz all serve people who compare options online first. When a customer searches for a service and a nearby area, they usually contact one of the first businesses Google shows.
