What Is Domain Authority in SEO and How Do You Improve It?

Learn what domain authority means, how Moz scores it, what counts as a good score, and how strong links lift it over time.

Domain authority gives you one score that predicts how well a site ranks. Moz built the score on a scale from one to one hundred. This guide explains what is domain authority in SEO and how you raise it.

Most beginners chase the score itself and buy cheap links to lift it fast. They ignore content quality and forget that trust grows slowly. This shortcut harms the site and wastes money.

You will finish with a clear plan to lift your score. You will learn the factors behind the number and the actions that move it. You will track progress month after month.

How Moz Calculates Domain Authority From Links And Trust

Moz calculates domain authority from the links that point to a site and the trust those links carry. Moz looks at linking site count, linking site strength, and link spread across many domains. A site earns a higher score when many trusted sites link to it from different places.

Moz also weighs the quality of each link far more than the raw count of links. One link from a respected news site outweighs dozens of links from weak blogs. This weighting explains why a small site with a few strong links can outrank a larger site with many weak links.

Ahmed from Lahore runs a small blog about home cooking and he watched his score rise after local food sites linked his recipes. He published one clear guide about bread baking and three local blogs cited it in their own posts. His score climbed slowly over the next few months and his recipes started ranking above older pages.

Why Domain Authority Differs From Domain Rating And PageRank

Domain authority comes from Moz, domain rating comes from Ahrefs, and PageRank comes from Google itself. This guide explains what is domain authority in SEO by placing it beside its rivals so you can read each score in its own context. You compare scores only within one tool because each tool uses its own data and scale.

Ahrefs builds domain rating mainly from the strength of backlinks, while Moz blends link quality with other trust signals. Google PageRank works inside Google ranking systems and Google never shows its current value to the public. You treat each score as a guide for comparison, never as a direct Google ranking factor.

Sara from Karachi manages a craft shop site and she once worried when her domain rating looked higher than her domain authority. She learned that the two tools measure different link sets and she stopped chasing one number to match the other. She focused on earning links from real craft blogs and both scores rose together over time.

What Makes A Good Domain Authority Score For Your Niche

A good score depends on your niche and the strength of rival sites. A score near thirty can win rankings in a small local niche, while a score near sixty may still struggle in a crowded global niche. You judge your score against the sites that rank for your own keywords, never against the whole web.

You check the scores of the top ten results for your main keyword and you note their range. You set your target just above the weakest site in that range so your goal stays realistic. This comparison keeps your effort focused on beating real rivals instead of chasing a round number.

Bilal from Islamabad runs a tuition centre site and he compared his score with other tuition sites in his city. He found that the top sites scored between twenty five and forty, so he set his target at forty five. He earned links from local education blogs and his score reached his target within a year.

How You Raise Domain Authority With Strong Backlinks

You raise domain authority by earning links from trusted sites in your own topic. You create a page that answers one question better than any rival page and you share it with writers who cover that topic. You ask for a link only when your page adds real value for their readers.

You also earn links through guest posts on respected blogs, mentions in local news, and helpful resources that other sites cite. This mix of methods supports what is domain authority in SEO in practice because it builds the trusted link profile that Moz rewards. You repeat the methods that earn links from strong sites and you drop the methods that earn only weak links.

Ayesha from Faisalabad runs a stitching business site and she earned links by writing a clear guide about bridal stitching. She shared the guide with local fashion bloggers and two of them linked it in their posts. She also spoke at a local business group and the group linked her guide on its resources page. Her score rose steadily and her site started ranking for local stitching searches.

How You Track Domain Authority And Fix Weak Spots

You track domain authority once a month with the same tool so your numbers stay comparable. You record your score, your linking sites, and your strongest new links in one sheet. You review the sheet each month and you spot the pages that earn links and the pages that earn none.

You then fix weak spots by improving thin pages, removing broken links, and building fresh links to pages that deserve them. You update old guides with new examples and you ask writers who linked an old page to link the updated version. This monthly cycle turns a static score into a working plan for growth.

Hassan from Multan runs a mobile repair blog and he tracked his score in a simple sheet every month. He noticed that his repair guides earned links while his news posts earned none. He shifted his time toward guides and he updated his older guides with clearer photos and steps. His score climbed over the next few months and local customers called his shop first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is domain authority?

Domain authority is a score from Moz that predicts how well a site can rank in search results. Moz builds the score from the links that point to a site and the trust those links carry. You use the score to compare your site with rivals, never as a promise of a ranking.

How can I check my domain authority score?

You can check your score with the free Moz Link Explorer tool by entering your site address. Moz shows your score, your linking sites, and your strongest pages in one report. You check the same tool each month so your trend stays clear.

Does Google use domain authority in its ranking?

Google does not use Moz domain authority in its ranking systems. Google reads links, content quality, and user signals directly when it ranks pages. You treat domain authority as a helpful guide for comparison, never as a Google factor.

What score should a new website aim for?

A new website should aim for a score near twenty five in its first year of steady work. The right target depends on the scores of the sites that rank for your keywords. You set a target just above your weakest rival so your goal stays reachable.

How long does it take to raise domain authority?

Most sites need several months of steady link earning to lift the score by a clear margin. The method behind what is domain authority in SEO rewards patience because trusted links build slowly. You speed up progress by earning links from strong sites in your own niche.

Do backlinks from social media raise domain authority?

Links from social media carry little weight for domain authority because most of them pass no link value. Social posts still help you reach writers who may link your page from their own sites. You use social media to spread useful pages, not as a direct source of authority.

Can a high domain authority score fall over time?

A score can fall when trusted links disappear, when rivals earn stronger links, or when your own site stops earning fresh links. Moz recalculates the score as the link data changes across the web. You protect your score by earning new links each month and keeping old pages useful.

Should I buy links to raise my domain authority fast?

You should never buy links to raise your score because bought links risk penalties and they often come from weak sites. Search engines can spot paid link patterns and they can lower trust in your site. You earn links through useful content and honest outreach instead.
