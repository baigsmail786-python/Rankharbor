How Beginners Use Ubersuggest for Keyword Research

Learn how beginners use Ubersuggest for keyword research. Find low difficulty keywords, read the difficulty score, and build a simple content plan.

how to use Ubersuggest for keyword research means you open the tool and you type a seed topic into the keyword search box. The tool shows related phrases with a difficulty score beside each phrase. You pick the phrases that match posts you can write with real knowledge.

Most beginners type a broad topic and they chase the phrase with the highest volume. They ignore the difficulty score and they pick phrases that big sites already own.

You will finish this guide with a short list of phrases that fit your site and your knowledge. You will read the difficulty score and choose topics that your site can win.

Start With a Seed Topic in the Keyword Search

You open Ubersuggest and you type a seed topic into the keyword search box at the top. The tool shows the volume and the difficulty score for that phrase in one clear view. You read those numbers first because they tell you if the phrase fits a new site.

You pick a seed topic that matches the posts you already write with real knowledge. A broad seed gives you a wide list and a narrow seed gives you a focused list. You start broad when you explore a new niche and you narrow the seed when you plan a single post.

Ayesha runs a small bakery blog in Lahore and she types soft naan recipe into the keyword search. She sees a list of related phrases and she notes the ones about soft naan and easy naan. She picks a phrase about soft naan at home and she writes a recipe post around it.

Read the Difficulty Score Before You Choose a Phrase

You read the difficulty score beside each phrase before you commit to a topic. The score runs from zero to one hundred and a low score means a new site can compete. You treat a score under thirty as a green light for a small site. You skip high scores until your site earns more links.

how to use Ubersuggest for keyword research also means you compare the difficulty score with the volume before you choose. A phrase with high volume and high difficulty wastes your effort on a new site. You pick phrases with modest volume and low difficulty because those phrases bring real visitors that you can win.

Bilal manages a clothing store site in Karachi and he checks the difficulty score for winter shawls. He finds a phrase about soft winter shawls with a low score. He writes a guide around that phrase and his product page gains visitors from search.

Use the Keyword Ideas Tab to Find Longer Phrases

You open the keyword ideas tab and you read the longer phrases that the tool suggests. These longer phrases match the exact words that searchers type into Google. You sort the list by difficulty and you scan the phrases at the top of the list. You save the phrases that match your knowledge and your products.

You look for phrases that name a clear problem or a clear result for the reader. A phrase like best soft naan recipe at home tells you what the reader wants. You write a post that answers that phrase in full because a full answer earns the click.

Sana writes travel guides in Faisalabad and she opens the keyword ideas tab for hill stations. She finds a long phrase about the best time to visit a hill station in winter. She writes a guide around that phrase and her page starts to receive visitors from search.

Compare Ubersuggest With Other Free Tools

how to use Ubersuggest for keyword research grows stronger when you compare its numbers with another free tool. You check the same phrase in a second free keyword tool and you compare the difficulty scores. You trust a phrase more when two tools show the same low difficulty.

You learn the limits of each tool so you use each one for its strength. Ubersuggest gives you quick ideas and clear difficulty scores for a beginner. A second tool may show different phrases that Ubersuggest misses. You collect ideas from both tools and you keep the best phrases in one sheet.

Usman runs a tuition centre site in Rawalpindi and he compares Ubersuggest with a second free tool. He finds that both tools show a low difficulty for a phrase about maths tuition fees. He writes a guide around that phrase and his course page gains visitors from search.

Turn Your Keyword List Into a Writing Plan

You turn your keyword list into a writing plan by sorting the phrases into groups by topic. You give each group one post and you match each phrase to a heading inside that post. You write the easiest group first and you set one post per week.

You write the post around the main phrase and you use related phrases in the headings. You answer the reader question in the first lines so the post earns the click. You link the post to your other posts on the same topic.

Hina manages a home decor blog in Multan and she sorts her keyword list into groups each month. She writes one post per week from the easiest group and she tracks the clicks in a simple notebook. She finds that her planned posts bring more visitors than her random posts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Ubersuggest and what does it do?

Ubersuggest is a keyword research tool that shows search volume and difficulty scores for phrases that people type into Google. You type a seed topic into the tool and you read a list of related phrases with their numbers. You use those phrases to plan posts that match real searcher intent. You start with the free version and you learn the tool before you consider a paid plan.

Is Ubersuggest free for beginners?

Ubersuggest offers a free version with a limited number of searches each day. You can run keyword searches and you can read difficulty scores without paying. You create a free account and you use the daily searches to learn the tool. You upgrade only when the daily limit blocks the work that your site needs.

What does the SEO difficulty score mean in Ubersuggest?

The SEO difficulty score runs from zero to one hundred and it shows how hard a phrase is to rank for in search. A low score means few strong sites compete for that phrase. A high score means strong sites with many links already own the top spots. You pick phrases with low scores when your site is new because a new site wins easier battles first.

How do I find low competition keywords in Ubersuggest?

You type a seed topic into the keyword search and you open the keyword ideas tab. You sort the list by difficulty and you read the phrases with the lowest scores. You check the volume beside each phrase and you pick the phrases that match posts you can write with real knowledge. You save those phrases in a sheet and you return to the sheet when you plan new posts.

How do beginners start keyword research with Ubersuggest?

how to use Ubersuggest for keyword research starts with a seed topic that matches your niche and your knowledge. You type the seed into the keyword search and you read the difficulty score beside each phrase. You pick phrases with low difficulty and modest volume and you save them in a simple sheet. You write one post per week from that sheet and you track the clicks in Search Console.

What is the Ubersuggest Chrome extension?

The Ubersuggest Chrome extension adds keyword data to your browser when you search on Google. You see the volume and the difficulty score beside the search results as you browse. You use it to check a phrase fast without opening the main tool. You install it from the Chrome Web Store and you sign in with your free account.

How is Ubersuggest different from Ahrefs for beginners?

Ubersuggest suits beginners because its free version gives quick ideas and clear difficulty scores with a simple layout. Ahrefs gives deeper data and stronger backlink tools but its free tools show less detail. You learn the basics with Ubersuggest and you move to Ahrefs when your site needs deeper checks. You compare the two tools on the same phrase and you keep the tool that fits your budget and your skill.

How often should I run keyword research in Ubersuggest?

You should run keyword research once each month and you should keep the session short and focused. You should pick one seed topic per group and you should save the best phrases in a sheet. You should write the posts from that sheet over the next four weeks. A monthly rhythm gives you enough ideas to plan ahead without wasting time on endless research.

how to use Ubersuggest for keyword research gives you a simple routine that a beginner can repeat each week. You type a seed topic and you read the difficulty score and you pick phrases that your site can win. You compare the numbers with a second tool and you turn the best phrases into a plan.

Open Ubersuggest today and find one low difficulty phrase for your next post.
