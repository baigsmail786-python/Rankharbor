Monthly SEO Campaigns

Short answer: A monthly SEO campaign is an ongoing programme that combines technical fixes, on-page and content improvements, and relevant link building, reported clearly every month. RankHarbor runs monthly campaigns for businesses in Pakistan and internationally, with published prices and no ranking guarantees.

Monthly SEO campaigns combine the services above into one sustained programme. SEO results compound: consistent technical health, content improvement and relevant link acquisition month after month is how durable organic growth is built.

How a monthly campaign runs

Each engagement follows a steady rhythm:

  • Initial assessment: audit, keyword map and competitor baseline
  • Monthly priorities agreed against the roadmap
  • Technical improvements implemented or briefed
  • Content optimization and new-content support
  • Link-building activity where appropriate
  • Progress report and monthly consultation call

Reporting & consultation

Your monthly report covers work completed, links earned, movement on tracked priorities and next month's plan — discussed on a consultation call, not just emailed as a PDF.

Monthly SEO Campaigns — quick answers

How much does monthly SEO cost in Pakistan?

RankHarbor's monthly SEO packages in Pakistan are PKR 25,000 (Starter), PKR 50,000 (Growth) and PKR 100,000 (Premium) per month. International packages are $150, $300 and $600 per month. The exact scope inside each package is confirmed in a written proposal.

What is included in a monthly SEO campaign?

Each month combines agreed priorities from your roadmap: technical improvements, content optimization and new-content support, link-building activity where appropriate, a work-completed report and a monthly consultation.

How long should a monthly SEO campaign run?

SEO needs time to compound, so we recommend judging a campaign over at least a quarter. Exact terms are set in your proposal, and monthly reports show the work completed and the next steps each cycle.
