Why Peshawar businesses need to be found on Google

Peshawar is a trading city on the old routes to the north and west, and its customers search on Google before they buy, book or visit. A dry fruit trader in Khyber Bazaar, a gemstone dealer near Namak Mandi, a transporter on University Road, a restaurant in Saddar, a clinic in Hayatabad and a retailer near Qissa Khwani Bazaar all serve people who compare options online first. When a customer searches for a product and a nearby area, they usually contact one of the first businesses Google shows. A business with a thin website and no local listings loses those customers to rivals who are easier to find. Peshawar customers often search with the market and area in the phrase, such as a dry fruit shop in Khyber Bazaar, a restaurant in Saddar Peshawar or a clinic in Hayatabad, so pages that talk about the real bazaars and neighbourhoods a business serves make a genuine difference.
