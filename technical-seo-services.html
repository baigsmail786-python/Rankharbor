What the audit covers

The audit examines the full technical foundation of your site:

  • Crawlability: robots.txt, crawl budget, orphan pages
  • Indexability: noindex usage, canonical tags, duplicate handling
  • XML sitemaps: accuracy, coverage and submission status
  • Redirects: chains, loops and status codes
  • Core Web Vitals and page experience signals
  • Structured data validation and eligibility
  • Internal linking and site architecture

Recommendations you can act on

Every finding is graded by impact and effort, written in plain language, and paired with the exact fix — so your developer can implement without guesswork, or we can discuss implementation support as part of your proposal.

Audit deliverables

You receive the findings report, a prioritized implementation checklist, and a walkthrough call to agree next steps.

Technical SEO — quick answers

How much does a technical SEO audit cost?

RankHarbor's preliminary audit is free. The Full SEO Audit — a complete technical, on-page, content and backlink audit with a prioritized action plan — is PKR 15,000 one-time. Technical SEO support can also be included in monthly packages.

What does a technical SEO audit include?

It covers crawlability and robots.txt, indexability and canonical tags, XML sitemap accuracy, redirect chains, Core Web Vitals, structured data validation, and internal linking and site architecture. Every finding is graded by impact and effort.

How often should a website get a technical SEO audit?

Stable sites should be audited about once a year, and again after a migration, redesign or major release. Sites with ongoing publishing or development usually need checks more often.
