Quick answers

SEO questions, answered directly

Short, straight answers to the questions people — and AI answer engines — ask most about SEO. No hype, no invented numbers, no ranking promises.

What is SEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) is the practice of improving a website so it appears higher in unpaid search results on Google and other search engines. It combines technical fixes, helpful content matched to search intent, and backlinks from relevant websites. SEO does not involve paying for ad clicks, and results build gradually over time.

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

AEO is the practice of structuring content so AI answer engines — such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity — can quote it directly as the answer to a question. It uses short, direct answers under question-style headings, clear definitions, and FAQ schema. AEO works alongside SEO, not instead of it.

How long does SEO take?

SEO usually takes several months of consistent work before meaningful ranking movement appears. The timeline depends on how competitive your market is, your site’s starting position and the scope of work. Anyone promising instant or guaranteed rankings is not being honest — Google controls its results, not any agency.

What does an SEO agency do?

An SEO agency audits your website, researches the keywords your customers search, fixes technical and on-page issues, creates or improves content, and earns relevant backlinks from other websites. It then reports the work completed and the results each month. RankHarbor does this for SaaS companies and growing businesses in Pakistan and internationally.

What is SEO link building?

Link building is the process of earning hyperlinks from other websites to yours. Search engines treat links from relevant, trusted sites as endorsements, which help your pages rank. Good link building is editorial and relevance-led — not buying links at scale.

What is SaaS link building?

SaaS link building earns backlinks specifically for software companies — from SaaS blogs, B2B publications, review sites and communities — targeting product, feature and content pages that drive trials and demos.

What is the difference between guest posting and link insertion?

A guest post publishes a brand-new article on another site containing your link. A link insertion adds your link contextually into an article that already exists on the host site. Both can work; the right choice depends on the page, topic and goal.

How do you evaluate publisher websites?

We check topical relevance, genuine audience and traffic signals, editorial standards, outbound link patterns, indexation of the linking page and pricing fairness. Sites that fail these checks are not proposed.
