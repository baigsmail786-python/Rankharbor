How Do Beginners Use Ahrefs Free Tools to Find Keywords and Backlinks?

Learn how to use Ahrefs free tools for SEO. Find keywords, check backlinks, audit your site, and run a 30 minute workflow with no paid plan.

Short answer: Ahrefs offers free tools that cover keyword ideas, backlink checks, site audits, and traffic checks, and you need no paid plan to start. You learn how to use Ahrefs free tools for SEO beginners by signing up for Webmaster Tools and running one tool at a time.

Most beginners open a paid SEO tool, see the price, and quit before they learn anything. They miss the free tools that answer the same first questions at no cost.

You will set up the free account, find keywords, check backlinks, and audit your site. You will finish with a 30 minute workflow you can repeat every week.

What the Free Ahrefs Tools Offer

Ahrefs gives away a set of free tools, and they cover the first questions every beginner asks. You audit your own site with Webmaster Tools, you find keyword ideas with the free Keyword Generator, and you check any domain with the free Backlink Checker. You read keyword difficulty, traffic estimates, and broken links without paying for a plan. You start with the free tools, and you learn the paid ones later when the work outgrows the free set.

Webmaster Tools Audits Your Own Site for Free

You sign up for Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, and you verify your own website to unlock the full free audit. You run Site Audit, and it lists the errors, warnings, and notices on your pages. You fix the errors first, because they block crawling or break the page for visitors. The Free Checkers Cover Keywords, Links and Traffic

You open the free tools page, and you find a checker for keywords, backlinks, traffic, and broken links. You paste a domain into the Backlink Checker, and you read the top links that point to it. You paste a seed word into the Keyword Generator, and you read a list of ideas with difficulty scores. A new agency that checked a rival with the free Traffic Checker found the pages that brought the rival its visits, and it built better versions of the same topics.

Find Keywords with the Free Keyword Generator

You find keywords with the free Keyword Generator, and you start with one seed word from your topic. You type the word, you pick Google as the search engine, and you read the list of ideas the tool returns. You sort the ideas by difficulty, and you keep the ones with low difficulty and a clear intent. You learn how to use Ahrefs free tools for SEO beginners by picking one keyword you can win, and you build the page around it.

Start with a Seed Word and Read the Ideas

You pick a seed word your buyers would type, and you enter it into the free Keyword Generator. You read the ideas it returns, and you look for phrases with three or more words, because long phrases carry clearer intent. You note the search volume and the difficulty beside each idea, and you shortlist the ones that match your page. A gardening shop that starts with indoor plants finds ideas like best indoor plants for low light, and each one fits a page.

Check Difficulty Before You Commit to a Keyword

You open the free Keyword Difficulty Checker, and you paste the keyword you want to target. You read the score, and you compare it with the strength of your own site. You pick a keyword with a low score when your site is new, because a strong rival page beats a weak page on a hard keyword. You save the hard keywords for later, and you win the easy ones first to build your traffic.

Check Backlinks Without a Paid Plan

You check backlinks with the free Backlink Checker, and you need no account to run it. You paste any domain into the tool, and you read the top hundred links that point to it. You see the linking page, the anchor text, and the strength of each link. You use the report to find link ideas for your own site, and you use it to watch the links your rivals earn.

Use the Free Backlink Checker on Any Domain

You open the free Backlink Checker, and you paste a rival domain into the box. You read the list of sites that link to it, and you sort them by strength to find the best ones. You open each linking page, and you ask whether your site could earn a link from the same place. A new agency that checked a rival found resource pages and directories it could join, and it earned its first links from that list.

Find Broken Links You Can Claim

You open the free Broken Link Checker, and you paste a rival domain into it. You read the list of dead pages on that site, and you note the sites that still link to them. You build a page that replaces the dead one, and you email the linking sites with the fix. A dead guide on composting once earned a new link for a gardening blog, because the blog offered a fresh page to replace it.

The 30 Minute Free Ahrefs Workflow

You tie the free tools together in one 30 minute session, and the routine covers keywords, links, and fixes in one pass. You start with a keyword idea, you check its difficulty, and you look at the pages that rank for it. You then check the backlinks of the top page, and you finish with a quick audit of your own site. You learn how to use Ahrefs free tools for SEO beginners by repeating this session weekly, and the habit beats a random check every time.

One Session from Keyword Idea to Backlink Check

You spend ten minutes on keywords, and you pick one idea with low difficulty from the Keyword Generator. You spend ten minutes on links, and you check the backlinks of the page that ranks first for that keyword. You spend ten minutes on your own site, and you run the Webmaster Tools audit to catch new errors. You write the three findings in a note, and you act on the clearest one before the next session.

Repeat the Workflow Every Week

You run the same session each week, and you compare the new numbers with the last week. You watch your keyword move, you watch your backlinks grow, and you watch the audit errors fall.
