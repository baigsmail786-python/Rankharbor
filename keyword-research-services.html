Keyword Research

Short answer: Keyword research finds the words and questions your audience actually searches, classifies them by intent, and maps each topic cluster to a page. RankHarbor delivers it as a prioritized content plan — not a raw spreadsheet of search volumes.

Good keyword research is a content plan, not a spreadsheet of volumes. We discover what your audience searches, classify it by intent, group it into topic clusters and map every cluster to a page — existing or new.

Our research process

Research combines multiple inputs so nothing important is missed:

  • Keyword discovery from seeds, competitors and Search Console
  • Search intent classification (informational, commercial, transactional)
  • Topic cluster building around your products and audience problems
  • Competitor keyword gap analysis
  • Content mapping: one primary intent per page
  • Prioritization by business value, intent fit and realistic difficulty

What you receive

A structured keyword and content map your team can execute for quarters — not a raw tool export.

Keyword Research — quick answers

What do I receive from keyword research?

You receive a prioritized keyword set with intent labels, a topic cluster and content map, a competitor gap summary, and briefing notes for priority pages — a plan your team can execute for quarters.

Do search volumes guarantee traffic?

No. Search volumes are estimates, and intent fit matters more than volume. RankHarbor prioritizes keywords by business value and realistic difficulty first, and volume last.

How much does keyword research cost?

Keyword research is included in RankHarbor's monthly SEO packages (PKR 25,000 to PKR 100,000 per month in Pakistan; $150 to $600 per month internationally), and a Full SEO Audit (PKR 15,000 one-time) includes a keyword and competitor overview.
